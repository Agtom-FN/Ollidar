CH340 USB serial driver
-----------------------

This installer does NOT redistribute WCH's CH341SER driver. It installs a local
page (drivers\CH340-driver.html) that explains how to check whether Windows'
in-box driver already covers your device and, if not, links WCH's own official
download. See that page for details.
