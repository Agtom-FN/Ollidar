LIDAR TEST - 3 STEPS
=====================

WHAT YOU NEED
  - The lidar (small silver/black spinning device) with its USB adapter
  - A Windows computer with internet

STEP 1  PLUG IN
  Plug the lidar's USB cable into the computer.
  First time only: wait about 1 minute (Windows sets it up by itself).

STEP 2  DOUBLE-CLICK "START_TEST"
  It is in this folder.
  If a blue window says "Windows protected your PC":
      click the small text "More info", then the button "Run anyway".
  A black window opens and counts to 30. The lidar should spin.

STEP 3  SEND THE RESULT BACK
  When the window says SUCCESS in green:
  a new folder called  LIDAR_TEST_RESULT  is on the Desktop.
  Send that folder back (WhatsApp / WeChat / email) - done!

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  The window explains what to try, in order.
  If it still fails: take a PHOTO of the window and send the photo.
